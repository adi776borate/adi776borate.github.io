I am an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar. Currently, I am a Visiting Undergraduate Researcher at California Institute of Technology (Caltech), advised by Dr. Ashish Mahabal, building an AI risk score to predict prostate cancer progression on active surveillance from 3D multimodal MRI. Previously, I was a Summer Undergraduate Research Fellow (SURF) at Caltech developing AI-readiness frameworks and Croissant-compliant data pipelines for medical imaging collections.

My research interests span medical AI and computer vision, with a focus on biomedical imaging, multimodal learning, and generative modeling. I am interested in developing AI methods that can understand, model, and recover information from complex visual data, ranging from scientific applications to generative vision and inverse problems.

Undergraduate Researcher — CVIG Lab, IIT Gandhinagar [Feb 2026 – Present]
Advisor - Prof. Shanmuganathan Raman, Akbar Ali | Poster
  • Investigated whether injecting lightweight semantic (DINOv3) and structural (PiDiNet) guidance directly into a frozen SDXL diffusion prior could eliminate the need for disjoint pre-cleaning networks in blind image restoration, training on 30K+ images across multiple real-world datasets.
  • Designed a two-phase training regimen consisting of coarse structural alignment via noise-prediction training over moderate-to-high noise timesteps with pixel-space regression at low-noise timesteps, followed by perceptual and semantic-similarity refinement with pixel-space losses gated to low-noise timesteps.
  • Discovered that continuous low-quality latent injection anchors the diffusion prior to the input, and formulated a Restoration Sampling Strategy (RSS) that thresholds this injection across the sampling trajectory achieving an SSIM of 0.50 under compound degradation.
Undergraduate Researcher — MUSE Lab, IIT Gandhinagar [Jan 2025 – Present]
Advisor - Prof. Himanshu Shekhar, Prof. Kenneth Bader | Presentation
  • Developing a real-time deep learning framework for automated segmentation of histotripsy-induced ablation zones in ultrasound imaging to enable treatment monitoring for renal tumors, using 1,280 B-mode frames from 13 RBC phantom studies in collaboration with the University of Chicago.
  • Designed causal, gain-invariant temporal features computed online from past frames that capture each pixel's running temporal variability, and trained an ImageNet-pretrained ConvNeXt U-Net with leave-one-sequence-out cross-validation that segments each frame in about 2 ms.
  • Outperformed prior state-of-the-art on the test set, with Dice 0.90 vs. 0.71 (+27%), mean IoU 0.90 vs. 0.75 (+20%), mean accuracy 0.94 vs. 0.80 (+17%), and Hausdorff distance 4.3 vs. 29.5 px (86% lower).
